About Us

Who are we ?

ShipDecKK is a premier shipbuilding company specializing in luxury yachts and commercial vessels. Founded in 2020, we are dedicated to crafting exceptional ships that blend elegance, performance, and innovation.

Our History

It All Started From A Dream

Born as the son of a humble carpenter in a quiet coastal town, Everton Deckk spent his childhood watching ships being built and repaired by hand. From a young age, he dreamed of crafting a private yacht—one that wasn’t just luxurious, but deeply personal and full of soul.

The First Boat, A Turning Point

Fueled by years of hands-on experience and a clear vision, Everton finally brought his dream yacht to life—a fully realized vessel that embodied elegance, innovation, and purpose. It wasn’t just a project; it marked the beginning of a new standard in personalized yacht design, capturing the attention of enthusiasts and professionals alike.

The Birth of ShipDecKK

In 2020, Everton turned his vision into a brand—Shipdeckk. More than a yacht company, Shipdeckk was founded to help others design and own personal yachts that represent who they are. With customization at its core, Shipdeckk invites dreamers to co-create vessels that are truly their own.

Vision

To be the world’s most trusted ship dealership, delivering excellence in every vessel and unmatched service in every voyage.

Mission

1. To provide premium-quality yachts, cargo ships, and luxury liners that meet the unique needs of every client.
2. To uphold the highest standards in craftsmanship by working with expert builders and using only the finest materials.
3. To deliver exceptional customer service that ensures long-term satisfaction and loyalty.
4. To support both leisure and commercial maritime ventures with reliable and innovative ship solutions.

Value

1. Excellence - We commit to delivering only the best, from ship quality to customer care.
2. Integrity - We conduct business with honesty, professionalism, and transparency.
3. Customer Focus - We put client satisfaction at the heart of everything we do.
4. Innovation - We embrace technology and craftsmanship to stay ahead in ship design and service.
5. Reliability - We ensure that every product and promise is dependable and lasting.
